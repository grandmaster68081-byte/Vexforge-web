# VEXFORGE Official Portal — reference research / V5

This document records the current public-facing reference patterns used for information architecture and visual hierarchy. It is maintainer documentation, not player-facing copy.

## Official TCG / card-game portal patterns reviewed

- Pokémon TCG — official TCG hub with featured cards, current product content and a clear route into the digital game. https://www.pokemon.com/us/pokemon-tcg/
- Magic: The Gathering — official portal with a strong promotional hero, then products, news, digital game, tabletop, competitive play and learning resources. https://magic.wizards.com/en
- Yu-Gi-Oh! TCG — official portal with clear top-level navigation for Products, Play, Events, News and beginner/rules resources. https://www.yugioh-card.com/en/
- Disney Lorcana — official game portal with product discovery, learning, community/events and collection-oriented surfaces. https://www.disneylorcana.com/en-GB/
- MARVEL SNAP — concise game-first navigation, large art-led promotional content, download CTA, news, media and support. https://marvelsnap.com/
- Riftbound — game introduction, product line, how-to-play, card gallery, events and organized-play progression. https://playriftbound.com/en-us/

## What V5.1 changes

The previous implementation read too much like a dark editorial microsite and, in the supplied mobile recording, the visible content included engineering/runtime information. V5.1 moves the first viewport toward established game-property patterns: large artwork, compact chrome, fewer words, stronger visual modules, clear product/category entry points, and mobile-specific proportions.

The portal intentionally does not copy any competitor's branding, artwork, copy, or layout one-for-one.
