# VEXFORGE V5.6 — REFERENCE RESEARCH

Research date: 2026-09-25.

The goal was not to copy another game's visual identity. The goal was to identify the recurring product/visual patterns used by major TCG/CCG portals and translate those principles into VEXFORGE's own dark-medieval language.

## Hearthstone
Official site emphasizes an immediately legible fantasy world, clear game proposition, modes, cross-device play, collection, rewards and a recurring content/news cadence. The portal should therefore communicate the game world before forcing the visitor through interface chrome.
Source: https://hearthstone.blizzard.com/en-us

## Magic: The Gathering Arena
The official Arena site combines cinematic fantasy imagery with explicit play modes, collection/deck building, customization, rewards and download access. It also maintains a strong current-content loop through expansion and event information.
Sources:
https://magic.wizards.com/en/mtgarena
https://magic.wizards.com/en/mtgarena/getting-started

## Pokémon TCG Pocket
The official portal gives the card object unusually high visual importance and explicitly highlights “immersive cards,” collection and the sensation of entering the card illustration. This supports making VEXFORGE's official cards the single canonical visual object while the environment art supports them.
Source: https://tcgpocket.pokemon.com/en-au/

## Shadowverse: Worlds Beyond
The official site combines card packs, characters, news/events and class-oriented worldbuilding in one visual ecosystem. This supports treating factions, collection and world as connected chapters rather than isolated pages.
Source: https://shadowverse-wb.com/

## Disney Lorcana
The official site treats cards, how-to-play, formats, organized play, collection and recurring content as parts of one continuing brand world. Its card gallery also demonstrates the value of a dedicated visual browse surface.
Sources:
https://www.disneylorcana.com/en-US/
https://cards.disneylorcana.com/en-US/
https://www.disneylorcana.com/en-US/play

## VEXFORGE translation
The common lesson is not “add more panels.” The common lesson is to make the game world, collectible objects, content cadence and entry points visually coherent. VEXFORGE therefore uses:

- monumental environmental scenes as the base layer;
- official cards as the only canonical remote visual asset;
- generated architecture/materials as the portal identity system;
- clear routes to game, cards, world, news and download;
- a collection surface that behaves like a gallery/shelf rather than a data grid;
- controlled motion that implies physical space;
- enough visual density that an empty content state still feels authored.
