# VEXFORGE V5.3 — REFERENCE RESEARCH

Research focus: live/current official TCG portals and their content architecture, not visual cloning.

## Hearthstone
Official pages emphasize the game fantasy, current card sets, modes and continuing news/cinematic content. The current site also presents card-set chronology and frequent expansion/update storytelling.

**Lesson applied:** VEXFORGE should make cards, factions and world content feel like first-class story objects rather than supporting copy.

Reference: https://hearthstone.blizzard.com/en-us

## Magic: The Gathering Arena
The current Arena portal puts recent sets above the fold, gives a clear “play/download” path, and moves into play modes, deck/collection building, rewards and news.

**Lesson applied:** a premium game property needs a visible path from identity → playable experience → collection → ongoing content.

Reference: https://magic.wizards.com/en/mtgarena

## Pokémon TCG Pocket
The official site makes collecting and “immersive cards” a core visual proposition and combines video, latest news and collecting information.

**Lesson applied:** let the card and its artwork carry emotion; do not bury it under UI chrome.

Reference: https://tcgpocket.pokemon.com/

## Marvel SNAP
The official site organizes the game around cards/characters, locations, game modes, videos and a direct free-play/download path.

**Lesson applied:** VEXFORGE can use short visual chapters around cards, places and modes instead of long text-only explanations.

Reference: https://marvelsnap.com/game-overview/

## GWENT
GWENT uses faction identity, multiple game modes, news, streaming/esports and a strong art-led presentation.

**Lesson applied:** factions can act as visual chapters with their own imagery and narrative signal.

Reference: https://www.playgwent.com/en/join

## Riftbound
Current Riftbound information architecture mixes product/set content, how-to-play, news, roadmap and organized-play communication.

**Lesson applied:** a growing TCG portal benefits from clear content pillars and an architecture that can absorb future live content without redesigning the visual system.

Reference: https://riftbound.gg/

## Digimon Card Game
The current official site relies heavily on product visuality, release cards, news, rules and organized-play updates.

**Lesson applied:** the portal must have durable content rails so new sets/cards/events can be added without breaking the visual identity.

Reference: https://world.digimoncard.com/

## Design conclusion for VEXFORGE

Do not copy any brand. Abstract the shared premium principles:

1. Art leads; copy supports.
2. Cards/characters/locations are visual objects.
3. Each chapter has its own composition and rhythm.
4. News and ongoing content are permanent architecture, not an afterthought.
5. Download/play access is always visible but does not require invented availability.
6. Mobile is designed as a visual browsing mode, not merely a collapsed desktop grid.
