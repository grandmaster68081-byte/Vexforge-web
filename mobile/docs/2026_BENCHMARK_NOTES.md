# 2026 Benchmark Notes

The reconstruction uses current public product information only as a quality benchmark, never as copied IP or copied rules.

## Master Duel
The official product page describes a competitive digital TCG, a ruleset comparable to Yu-Gi-Oh!, tournaments/events, a very large card pool, cross-platform progression and a presentation built around HD graphics. VEXFORGE should meet the discipline of clear rules, deep deckbuilding and strong audiovisual feedback without copying its IP or rules.

## MTG Arena
Wizards describes MTG Arena as a mobile/desktop CCG with rules fidelity to Magic, multiple constructed formats, deck legality, ranked play, events, progression and collection growth. VEXFORGE should similarly treat rules, deck construction, progression and long-term content as first-class systems.

## Legends of Runeterra
The 2026 update cadence still includes new champions, adventures, encounters, mini-bosses, bosses and reward structures. This supports treating VEXFORGE PvE as a real progression layer rather than a cosmetic side menu.

## Marvel Snap
VEXFORGE's visual protocol already uses the broader principle that cards should dominate the visual hierarchy and that mobile composition should be authored around cards and touch. The new runtime keeps this principle while retaining VEXFORGE's own formation, faction, economy and world systems.
