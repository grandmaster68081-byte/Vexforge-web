# VEXFORGE · 2026 TCG Benchmark Discipline

These are references for production discipline, not templates or copied mechanics.

- Marvel Snap demonstrates the value of cards as the visual center of the experience, strong mobile composition and fast, readable battle states.
- Yu-Gi-Oh! Master Duel demonstrates deep rules fidelity, a large card pool, competitive play, tournaments and a highly presentation-driven digital duel client.
- MTG Arena demonstrates the importance of rules fidelity, formats, deck legality, collection growth, ranked play and long-term live-service content.
- Legends of Runeterra demonstrates how a card game can extend beyond PvP into adventure encounters, bosses and reward structures.

VEXFORGE should borrow the underlying discipline: readable complex state, meaningful deckbuilding, strong card identity, replayable progression, event-driven feedback and live-service content. It must keep its own rules, universe, assets and economy.
