# Kivora — Brand Identity

## Name

**Kivora**

Pronunciation: **ki-VO-ra**.

Kivora is the independent rewards/earn platform that lives inside the Vexforge-web repository but does not reuse the VEXFORGE game's application, identity, authentication or data contracts.

## Positioning

Kivora is a modern advertiser-funded rewards platform. The public product language should focus on verified opportunities, transparent reward accounting and user-controlled withdrawals. Avoid describing rewards as guaranteed income or guaranteed profit.

## Primary tagline

**Turn attention into value.**

Supporting line:

**Complete verified opportunities. Stack Kivora Points. Choose when to cash out.**

## Visual direction

- Dark, premium, mobile-first interface.
- High-contrast text and restrained glow, not a casino aesthetic.
- Cyan-to-violet signal gradient for actions and highlights.
- Soft glass panels with visible borders and generous spacing.
- Local SVG assets only; no dependence on hotlinked brand art.
- Motion should reinforce state changes, reward confirmation and navigation rather than flashing or distracting the user.

## Product vocabulary

Use:

- Kivora
- Kivora Points
- Earn
- Opportunities
- Rewards
- Wallet
- Withdraw
- Pending / Available / Confirmed

Avoid:

- "free money"
- guaranteed earnings
- guaranteed crypto profit
- fake scarcity
- fabricated payout claims

## Brand clearance

This package establishes **Kivora** as the working product identity. Before public commercial launch, perform a trademark, business-name and domain clearance in the jurisdictions where the platform will operate. This repository package does not certify availability of the name or any domain.

## 2026 product pattern baseline

The product intentionally uses current rewards-platform interaction patterns without copying another product: a multi-category Earn Hub, visible payout before starting an opportunity, search/filter/sort, activity history, progress/levels, daily progress signals, a unified points balance, and an explicit reviewed cashout flow. Current 2026 examples such as Freecash, JumpTask and Cointiply expose combinations of games/offers/surveys/tasks/videos, visible rewards, unified balances, cashout methods, streaks, goals or levels; Kivora adapts these patterns into its own interaction model and visual language.
